# Consulting reframes

Mentor the firm’s work. Do not mentor his character. Teaching as a consultant is a skill on the artifact. Lecturing is a verdict on the person.

If the note could be said to any client on this deliverable, it is teaching. If it only makes sense because of *him*, it is parenting.

---

## What it hits in him

A lecture is not a correction to him. It is a 7th-house meal. You think you are pushing against red. His chart hears: **I am still in relatedness, I am being parented, I can survive this with words.** The file does not have to move.

**伤官 luck (甲)**

So he produces insight, context, therapy-language, a smarter story. He can even agree. Agreement is a 伤官 tactic: *if I understand it, I don’t have to submit to it.* You get a good conversation. He gets out of being held.

**Venus in Gemini in the 7th**  
Talk is glue. Lecturing when he is wrong **keeps the bond active at the exact moment the ledger should be the only object.** Fog, delay, charm-for-credit all get a scene. The red is rewarded with your mind, your time, your heat. Gemini does not need the content to be kind. A scolding is still a dialogue.

**Virgo Moon in the 10th**  
He already lives in competence-shame. A lecture lands as **character review of the 10th**: you are not good enough at the thing you use to exist. Typical outputs: freeze, pick apart the work, go extra-correct in tone and still late on cash, or a tight “you’re right” that is really *get me out of this feeling.* Shame does not become a transfer. It becomes weather in the 4th (Pisces Mars) and then silence or nitpick.

**Saturn on the lagna**  
He can **look** like he is taking it — serious, sorry, I’ll do better. That is Saturn’s face. Then he slow-walks. The lecture gave him a **duty performance** (the apology) as a substitute for the dull Saturn job (the date, the amount).

**偏财**  
Attention is the tap. Negative attention still counts. The capable woman is **engaged**. For 偏财 that is not punishment; it is proof the option is alive. Mood-pay and charm-for-credit are more likely **after** a lecture, not less — the swamp wants to restore relatedness.

**Pisces Mars in the 4th**  
A lecture is more flood, not a shoreline. Unlocated Mars + being told he is wrong → vanish, collapse, or a swamp-fight (everything at once, nothing dated). Radio silence after you “finally said it” is often this, not a mystery.

**What it does to the goal**

| You intend | He often learns |
|---|---|
| Stop the red | Reds that produce a scene still work |
| Hold him accountable | Words discharge accountability |
| Make business matter more | Business became a couples topic |
| He’ll feel the cost | He felt *you*; the invoice did not change |

On this wiring, **cost that changes behavior is: the shop does not move.** Cost that does not change it is: a true, intelligent, deserved speech. Anything that needs him to understand he was wrong is still a class. He is very good at class. He is not using that skill on the transfer.

## Instead of (parent / lecture) → say (consultant / partnership)

- You’re unreliable. → This timeline slipped twice; prepaid blocks stop that on the next one.
- You always do this. → This file has the same bottleneck as the last: dates with no owner.
- You never follow through. → Monday’s yes is what I’m holding unless you change it in writing.
- You need to grow up / get healthier. → Firms usually split this: one person owns dates, one owns the draft.
- That’s irresponsible. → Scope, date, or pause until the retainer — which constraint are we using?
- Do you see the pattern? → The pattern in this deliverable is X; the fix that tends to work is Y.
- You’re letting your relationship run the company. → I need work decisions in the work channel, dated.
- We need to talk about how you show up. → Here’s the number due on [date]. Work stays paused until it clears.
- I need you to understand this. → Two options are in the doc; pick one for this block.
- Does that make sense? → I’ll put that in the file.
- Why didn’t you just pay / write / decide? → What’s the constraint on this deadline?
- You’re attacking the work because you’re ashamed. → Let’s stay on original scope and the number we named.
- Be better. → Next block starts when Y is in; this block’s toy is [the piece].
- This is about your character. → This is about this clause / this process / this draft.
- Get it together so we can be partners. → Partnership is the system: written yes, owner of dates, money before labor.
- I’m disappointed in you. → Cut scope, move the date, or pause — those are the levers.
- You should want to change. → You can use this method on the next file or not; I’m using it on mine.
- Let’s process what happened. → Ten minutes on the model, then we make.
- Are we okay? → Is this block a sandbox or is it due Friday?
- I need you to learn from this. → Same mistake = same mechanism next time (prepay / CC / pause). No extra speech.

## Hats

| Role | Use |
|---|---|
| Teacher | A tool on this page, then stop. |
| Mentor | One reusable move for the firm, not a curriculum of him. |
| Partner | Peers on the puzzle; the record is the doc. |
| Parent | Tracking whether he became someone else — drop this hat. |

## Split the rooms

**Room A — money and dates (boring, written, once)**  
Amount, date, paused until X, next block starts when Y is in. No why, no character. Send it; stop.

**Room B — the craft (only if Room A is not in dispute)**  
Paid or prepaid. The task is the piece, not his reliability. Talk like two people making something. If you hear yourself diagnosing *him*, you have left Room B.

If Room A is still open (owed, reversed, fog), there is no Room B.

---

## How to point things out

1. Point at the object; name the pattern **in the object**.
2. Externalize (“firms usually…”).
3. One teachable per pass.
4. Options, then stop.
5. Ask only about the piece.
6. Time-box the mentor hat (ten minutes: model + example, then make).
7. Do not wait for the learning face. Close with: “I’ll put the two options in the doc.”

**Test:** Would you send this exact note to a client you do not care about personally, attach it to the file, and go to lunch? If yes, teach it. If you need him to feel it, don’t.
